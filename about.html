OUR GLASS is the software home of The Afterword Concierge, a developing service for preparing farewell wishes and keeping the people who matter informed.

What is OUR GLASS?

OUR GLASS is being designed as a private space where a person can plan their afterword: the messages, practical instructions and people they want remembered when the time comes.

The first planned service is Death Notification & Beyond. It is intended to help someone prepare trusted contacts and, after appropriate verification, send death notifications to the people they chose.

How is it intended to work?

A person would set up their wishes and invite trusted recipients to share contact information. When the person dies, an authorized initiator would request activation. The planned workflow includes checks before notifications can be sent.

Donations for setup, payment verification, private account access and notification delivery are being built and tested. The website's introductory pages do not yet provide these completed services.

Can I register or donate now?

Not yet. Account registration and payment-proof submission are not open to the public. Please do not send a donation for OUR GLASS account setup until the verified submission process is available on the official website.

What about privacy?

Farewell instructions, trusted contacts and payment records require careful handling. The service is being designed with permission-based sharing, private records and human review for sensitive steps. A full privacy notice and the necessary security controls will be published before personal-data collection begins.

What does “& Beyond” mean?

Death notification is the starting service, not a promise that every future feature is available today. The Afterword Concierge is intended to grow carefully into related ways of preparing, preserving and sharing farewell instructions.
