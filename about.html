Death Notification & Beyond

For the things we mean to say, and the people we never want to leave wondering.

An afterword, with intention.

OUR GLASS is the software home of The Afterword Concierge, a thoughtful approach to preparing personal wishes, important messages and the people who matter most.

Some conversations deserve more care than a hurried message. The Afterword Concierge brings clarity and consideration to the difficult, deeply human moment of saying goodbye.

You decide what matters.

Your words.

The messages, memories and instructions that carry your wishes, expressed in your own voice.

Your people.

A carefully chosen circle of family, friends and others you want to keep informed, with respect for their privacy.

A trusted handoff.

A designated person and a verification-first process bring intention and accountability to the moment an afterword is shared.

Beyond a notification.

Death Notification & Beyond places the first message within the wider care of communicating your wishes and what follows.

Care is in the details.

Privacy, discretion and the dignity of the people receiving the news are at the heart of The Afterword Concierge.
