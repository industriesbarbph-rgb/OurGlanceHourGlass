What we leave behind is more than a message. It's a chance to care for our people, even when we can no longer be there.

A thoughtful way to prepare.

OUR GLASS is the digital home of The Afterword Concierge, a service being created for people who want to make their wishes known and help their chosen people receive important news with care.

It's about making room for conversations that are often postponed, and helping the people we love find clarity at a difficult moment.
